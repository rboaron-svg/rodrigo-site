How I got here

At 16 I redesigned Venice's MOSE storm barrier for my A Level 3D Design, as a movable barrier based on the Thames Barrier, and built a scale model to see it close across a channel. Working through it showed me how much civil engineering can do, including protecting people in coastal zones. That's why I'm studying it, why I'm taking Ocean Engineering as a minor, and why I later gave a Student Society talk on the problems MOSE faces.

Since then I've shadowed a road engineer on an airport design team in Milan. At UCL I've modelled a building in Revit from a laser scan and designed a car park for a London site. All of it is in my portfolio.

I notice what's off in a design and stay on it until it's fixed.
