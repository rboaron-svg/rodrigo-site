Revit and AutoCAD

Two projects from first-year coursework at UCL: modelling a building from survey data, and designing a car park on a real London site.

Building model from a laser scan

ProjectRevit model of St David’s Building, built from a laser scan
SoftwareRevit
SourceCourse scan data
ContextUCL, first year
ModelWalls, floors, windows, doors

First-year coursework at UCL: build a complete 3D model in Revit of the exterior of St David's Building, its quadrangle and façade, from a laser-scan point cloud. The brief set exterior walls, doors and windows as the minimum, with extra credit for other details found in the point cloud.

Revit was new to me, so most of the effort went into getting used to the tools. With more time I would go beyond that minimum and model the courtyard and the detailed design of the tower.

Car park design for a London site

ProjectCar park design for the Here East site, London
SoftwareAutoCAD, FME, ArcGIS Pro
Site dataOS MasterMap, aerial imagery
ContextUCL, first year
Result96/100

For first-year CAD and GIS coursework I designed a car park on a site next to a building area, using the standard bay and aisle dimensions from the brief. I drew it in AutoCAD, placed it on the site drawing, converted it with FME and mapped it in ArcGIS Pro in 2D and 3D. It includes standard, electric and disabled bays, bike bays, green space, a one-way road and a pedestrian walkway.
