Coastal design

The project that made me want to study civil engineering.

Movable storm barrier

ProjectMovable storm barrier, a redesign of Venice's MOSE
StatusScale model built
Drawn byR. Boaron Otero
BriefA Level 3D Design
Age16

I redesigned Venice's MOSE storm barrier as a movable barrier based on the Thames Barrier, then built a scale model to see it close across a channel. What pulled me in was solving the problem across all its parts, at small scale.

Working through it showed me how much civil engineering can do, including protecting people in coastal zones. It is why I'm studying civil engineering, why I'm taking Ocean Engineering as a minor, and why I later gave a Student Society talk on the problems MOSE faces.
