Planning opportunity finder

ProjectLondon planning opportunity finder, a tool to spot design work early
StatusBeta
RoleBuilder
DataGLA Planning London Datahub
AimFind projects early

The idea is that a consultancy can bid better if it hears about a project early. This tool reads new London planning applications and ranks them by how likely they are to need a small or mid-size design consultancy for structural, civil or drainage work. Every score shows its reasons, and every rejected application shows why it was dropped.

The beta snapshot covers 4,083 live applications from June to October 2026 across eight boroughs. It is a rule of thumb, not a verdict: the data never names the engineer on a scheme, so you still have to open the documents before treating anything as winnable.
